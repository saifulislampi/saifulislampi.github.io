  3. eScience
    Portable By Design: Deploying Notebook-based Scientific Workflows across HPC Clusters
    Md Saiful Islam, Talha Azaz, Raza Ahmad, and 6 more authors
    In 2026 IEEE 22nd International Conference on eScience (eScience), Poster Paper, 2026
    Abs Bib HTML

    Notebooks are the default interface for exploratory science, but scaling them into distributed HPC workflows remains hard: provisioning and data staging are usually hand-coded into notebook cells, so the same notebook rarely runs unmodified elsewhere. We present an ecosystem that externalizes these concerns instead of burying them in code. TaskVine supplies scalable, fault-tolerant distributed execution. A backpack captures a notebook’s software, data, and compute requirements as a single portable specification. Floability Audit observes a workflow’s execution and automatically turns it into a backpack. Floability then provisions the backpack unmodified across heterogeneous HPC sites, resolving and caching data through a declarative data layer. Together, these pieces make portability a property of the ecosystem rather than a discipline imposed on the notebook.
